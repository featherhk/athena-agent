# Companion owl click regression

The installed build 20 could expand and collapse successfully at rest, but the
transition displaced the owl and input. Settled screenshots did not establish
whether the intervening frames were stable.

A production React/WKWebView regression samples the controls on every animation
frame, starting before a native click and continuing through the native resize.
Coordinates are relative to the panel bottom, which the real companion preserves
while growing upwards. This checks content layout; it does not measure compositor
frame rate or native window origin behavior.

Before the fix, the first opening frame (13 ms after the click) displaced both
controls down by 36 CSS pixels. The input's bottom inset changed from 14 to -22,
so it was briefly clipped. The DOM nodes remained identical and only one resize
was requested. The result's minimum padding, border and margin overflowed the
compact panel before the debounced native resize arrived.

The fix bottom-aligns the existing flex column. Temporary overflow now extends
upwards, preserving the controls' positions without remounting them, changing the
window protocol, or adding a second visibility state. Coverage includes running
and idle results, repeated short/long-result toggles, retained drafts, unchanged
input identity, and no accidental submissions or stops.

The corrected production WKWebView probe passed all 93 checks. Across 14 owl
toggles (393 sampled frames), maximum control displacement was 0 pixels, with
one resize per toggle. Two pre-existing fixture steps now wait for native size
settlement before an unrelated coordinate click or background-status baseline;
the click-regression sampling itself still starts before the click and does not
skip intermediate frames. The one-pixel assertion was not relaxed.

Installation and final acceptance are recorded after validating the candidate.
